About This Curriculum

This 12-lesson, 5-day curriculum was written to give practitioners and learners a structured path into maritime operational technology security — the systems that keep ships moving and ports running, from the bridge and engine room to the container yard. It pairs technical material with worked examples, hands-on activities, and browser-based simulations, and leans on the maritime-specific frameworks (IACS UR E26/E27, IMO's maritime cyber risk guidance) alongside the general OT standards (NIST SP 800-82, IEC 62443, MITRE ATT&CK for ICS) that a maritime OT security role actually needs.

Scope & Intent

Every lab and simulation in this course runs against simulated data — none of it is designed to interact with a live vessel, port terminal, or production network. The full policy is documented on the Safeguards & Responsible Use page, and it applies to every lesson linked from the course navigation.
